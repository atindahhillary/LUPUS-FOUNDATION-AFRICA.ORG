LFA Shop

Wear the message. Carry the cause.

Hoodies, T-shirts and notebooks carrying the Lupus Warrior seal. Proceeds support peer support groups, awareness and patient assistance across Africa, and every one starts a conversation about lupus.

Every purchase supports the Lupus Foundation of Africa and its work with people living with lupus across Africa.
