Partner with LFA

Together, we can build a future where lupus is diagnosed earlier

Improving the lives of people living with lupus requires collaboration across the health and social ecosystem. If your organisation can help, we would like to talk.

Partnership opportunities

Ways to work with us

Corporate partnerships

Support awareness campaigns, patient programmes or chapter activities, and give your teams a cause with a visible, local impact.

Healthcare partnerships

Hospitals and clinics working with us on awareness, earlier recognition, referral pathways and access to specialist care.

Research partnerships

Universities, registries and research institutions building African lupus evidence with patients as partners.

Media partnerships

Broadcasters and publishers helping carry patient voices and accurate lupus information to a wide audience.

Pharmaceutical partnerships

Work with us toward greater access to affordable, appropriate lupus treatment and patient education.

Foundation & donor partnerships

Multi-year support for programmes across awareness, patient support, advocacy and research.

Event sponsorship

Sponsor World Lupus Day, a wellness day, a chapter meet-up or a community outreach activity.

In-kind support

Venues, printing, transport, diagnostics, professional services, equipment or expertise all make a real difference.

Employee giving

Payroll giving, matched giving and team fundraising that build a sustained base of support.
