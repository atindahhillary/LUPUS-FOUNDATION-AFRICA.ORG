Donate

Support the lupus community

Every contribution reaches a real person: someone waiting for a diagnosis, someone choosing between medicine and rent, someone who has just been told they have lupus and does not know anyone else who has.

What your gift does

Your contribution helps us

  • Reach more people with lupus, including those who have never heard the word
  • Provide patient support through peer groups and psychosocial care
  • Increase awareness so that diagnosis comes years sooner
  • Advocate for better healthcare, coverage and access to medicines
  • Support community activities across our chapters
  • Amplify patient voices in research, media and policy

I take about eight prescription medications daily; that gets very expensive even with insurance. Some weeks are filled with doctor visits or tests, which become very tiresome and expensive.

Steve Okuku, Finance Manager, LFA

How to give

Making your donation

To make a donation, or to set up regular giving, please contact our team directly. We will confirm the current payment options, including mobile money and bank transfer, and send you a receipt.

We deliberately do not publish payment details on this page. Donation pages are a common target for impersonation, and we would rather you confirm the details with us directly than trust a number on a website.

Call +254 142 851 978 Email info@lupusfa.org WhatsApp us

Always verify before you send money. LFA will only ever confirm payment details through the phone number or email address published on this website. If someone contacts you claiming to collect donations for LFA, please check with us first on +254 142 851 978.

Accountability

Where your giving goes

We publish our impact figures annually and review them with our members. Annual reports, audited accounts and governance documents are available to donors and partners on request.

If you give to LFA, you are entitled to know what happened to it. Ask us and we will tell you.
