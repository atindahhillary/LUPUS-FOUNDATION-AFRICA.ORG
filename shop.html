LFA Shop

Wear the message. Carry the cause.

Hoodies, T-shirts and notebooks carrying the Lupus Warrior seal. Proceeds support peer support groups, awareness and patient assistance across Africa, and every one starts a conversation about lupus.

How ordering works

Simple, personal and safe

Every order is confirmed by a real person on our team before you pay, so you always know exactly what you are paying for.

01

Add to cart

Choose your items and sizes. Your cart is saved on this device while you browse.

02

Check out

Tell us where to deliver, or collect in Nairobi, and choose how you would like to pay.

03

We confirm

We call or WhatsApp you from +254 142 851 978 to confirm sizes, delivery and payment.

04

Pay and receive

Pay by M-Pesa, card or on collection, then we deliver or you pick up.

Shop questions

Before you order

We will only ever contact you about an order from +254 142 851 978 or info@lupusfa.org. Please never send money to any other number.
